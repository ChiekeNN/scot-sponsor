# Sponsorship capability audit — 31 October 2026

Existing features are distinguished from illustrative tools, editorial resources and capabilities not yet supported. Listings, employer quotas, immigration permission and sponsorship are not inferred from sponsor-register presence. Neither missing information nor a checklist result is a negative finding. The Career Journal is public; planning tools are inside the member gate.

1. **CoS allocation radar:** not available. Employer-specific Defined/Undefined allocations are not disclosed in the public sponsor register. Journal explains the distinction; employer data or explicit authorisation would be required.
2. **Immigration Skills Charge:** journal explains conditional exemptions and links to GOV.UK. It does not identify employers or decide exemption; this depends on worker and role facts and current rules.
3. **Insolvency sentinel:** existing Companies House lookup and daily admin sponsor-register change monitor are different tools, not an insolvency alert. Journal signposts Companies House and The Gazette. Immediate user alerts need reliable data, exact-entity matching, subscriptions and delivery infrastructure.
4. **Care Inspectorate / SSSC:** journal signposts official checks. No live ratings, worker registration matching or filter; accurate service-level mapping and current authoritative data are needed.
5. **Ghost jobs:** new self-check questions only. No automatic detection or removal; that requires reliable evidence, review, appeals and abuse controls.
6. **Scottish salary:** existing 2026/27 income tax and NI estimator plus budget. Budget now accepts manually estimated monthly pension take-home deductions and visa savings; it does not model payroll pension tax relief or calculate visa fees, student loans, or individual tax codes.
7. **Student hours:** new account-scoped, local-browser manual log with Monday–Sunday aggregation, user-entered dates and conditions. Not a university calendar integration or eligibility determination; storage is not encrypted and cross-device sync is unavailable.
8. **SOC matcher:** existing authenticated official title/code search and standard/lower rates. Description matching and undercoding assessment are not available; no conclusions should be made from keyword overlap.
9. **Processing benchmarks:** unavailable. A trustworthy version needs consent, verified submissions, sample-size thresholds, privacy, moderation and a clear no-precision guarantee.
10. **Evidence:** new client-side encrypted single-file backup/download using AES-GCM and PBKDF2, with no server storage, sync, verified timestamp or recovery. It is not an independently audited cloud document vault.
11. **In-country switching filter:** unavailable. Sponsor licence or a job advert alone does not establish an employer’s willingness to sponsor an in-country switch; employer-confirmed, current vacancy data is needed.
12. **University-to-sponsor outcomes:** unavailable; needs permissioned and reliably attributed historical graduate outcomes, privacy controls and appropriate cohort denominators.
13. **NHS boards:** journal links to the official recruitment site and explains the boards. A live sponsorship vacancy dashboard needs licensed current vacancies and vacancy-specific evidence of sponsorship.
14. **Highlands and Islands:** journal suggests direct checks. Matching jobs to real relocation support requires current verified local programmes and role-level benefits.
15. **Settlement:** new five-year calendar scenario and links to official guidance; no absence compliance, settlement salary or eligibility determination. Rolling absences, qualifying routes, application timing and future rules require professional assessment.
16. **SME readiness:** new self-guided checklist and current official sources. Not a licence or legal-compliance assessment.
17. **Fair Work First / Real Living Wage badges:** unavailable. Needs authoritative accreditation data, identity matching, currency, scoped claims and revocation handling; no badge inferred from an employer name.
18. **Legal help:** journal links to the official immigration adviser finder and Law Society search, including legal aid; no locally vetted pro-bono directory or guarantee of current service availability.
19. **Founder resources:** new journal article links to Innovator Founder rules, Scottish Business Gateway and regulated advice, without asserting eligibility.
20. **Exploitation heatmap:** unavailable. Sensitive geographic reporting needs consent, verification, safeguards against re-identification, small-number suppression, moderation and an appropriate data governance framework. No reports or risk scores have been fabricated.
